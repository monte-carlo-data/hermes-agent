  Deliberately free of `lookup`: reading cluster state would make this render
  differently under `helm template`, `--dry-run`, and the Terraform helm
  provider's plan phase, and would require `get secrets` on the caller. Naming
  what the release expects is enough. See `hermes.oauth.enabled` and
  `hermes.auth.source` in _helpers.tpl for how the method and source are
  selected.

  Kept short on purpose: install output competes for attention with whatever
  the operator was actually doing. What the release authenticates with, the one
  prerequisite the chart cannot satisfy itself, and how to check it. Anything
  conditional on the operator having misconfigured something belongs in the
  README, where it can be read without being in the way.
*/}}
